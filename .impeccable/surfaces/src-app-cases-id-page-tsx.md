# Case page (surface brief)

Scope: `/cases/[id]` with `?tab=overview|material|source|tasks|history` and `?part=<letter>` (URLs unchanged), mode **Operate**. Audience: the accessioning lead and supervisor following one case end to end; bench staff who open it from a scan; the Director of Operations and medical director, read only, in the guided demo. Task: see one case at a glance: every part with where it physically is and where its documentation is (a case is never shown complete while any part is incomplete), what is held and why, who holds which role, the source documents with every version (simulated source), open tasks and clarifications, and the append-only history. Constraints: PRODUCT.md and docs/redesign/visual-system.md; queries stay read-only (derived fields only); task claim/release keep their server rules; synthetic and simulated labelled once where they matter; no confidence numbers, no invented rates; locations are last confirmed, never live. Baseline problems (shots/other-case-1366): a page header plus a stack of equal cards (Case status, People by role, one bordered card per part with two checklists), the status sentence buried at text-sm inside a card, the hold a separate banner far from the part it holds, the 8-step physical checklist laid out as two columns of circles.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The parts are the subject. One evidence sheet lists every part as its own specimen label, with its physical path and its documentation path drawn as two short tone rails, so "where is it and what is it waiting for" reads in one sweep. It refuses the dashboard of equal cards and the checklist of circles.
OWN-WORLD: The shared H&E world, lent unchanged: hematoxylin ink and shell, eosin only for focus, cool bench-gray ground, white evidence sheets, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for words and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono only for identifiers, the severity ladder (hold as hatched ink on the part and on the exact step it stops, amber with words for a failed source update or a step that needs clarification, waiting neutral with the owner's name, PAS written and yellow, done quiet), and the specimen-label signature leading each part row.
STORY: The lead reads one sentence ("Not complete: 0 of 3 parts delivered, 1 part on hold."), then each part's label, its two rails (done quiet, current filled, next outlined, held hatched), and on the right who it waits on. The director sees the same calm structure, read only, and every other tab speaks the same language: the shared Table for material, order lines and tasks; the shared Timeline for history.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (lead's); sticky IdentityBar led by the case label; the case band (h1 with the case facts, the focal status sentence at xl with the readiness line under it, people by role as a compact label/value list on the right, the section tabs on its lower edge); then the parts sheet, one row per part: label and site on the left, physical rail (8 steps) and documentation rail (4 steps) in the middle, status and owner on the right; a hold is a hatched strip inside its part's row.
FORM: Extends the Intake world. User-pinned: the user's instruction for this redesign reads "Focus first on making the accessioning workstation excellent. Keep the active case and specimen identity visible, align evidence for comparison, and make the next action clear. Then extend the selected design language to the other screens." The selected language is the Intake H&E workstation (concept A, chosen on the brief's ordering). No concept-seed roll ran for this screen, by design: the user asked for extension of one selected language, not a new direction. Screen-specific form: the part ledger with two step rails per part (Intake's finding-band rail, read only), counts aligned in a small expected/prepared/scanned table, order lines aligned original/current/on-case.
FINISH: Captured at 1440, 1366 and 1024 and at 200% zoom (720x450, 683x384) in every required state (overview, material, sources, tasks, history, a held case, a PAS case, read-only director, not found), looked at, fixed and recaptured once; tsc and the case domain tests green; the case-page e2e assertions in director-journey steps 4 and 5 updated to the new structure at equal strength.
