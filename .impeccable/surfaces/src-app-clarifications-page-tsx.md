# Clarifications desk (surface brief)

Scope: `/clarifications` with `?tab=my_open|overdue|awaiting_office|needs_review|resolved|all` and `?selected=<CLR id>` (URLs unchanged; a scanned or linked CLR with no tab falls back to All), mode **Operate**. Audience: Kim Nguyen (accessioning lead: reviews answers, resolves, releases holds), Maya Torres (accessioner: sends drafts, records answers given by phone, follows up), the read-only director in the guided demo. Task: specimen-specific questions to submitting offices, from draft to a reviewed resolution: see what is overdue or failed to deliver, what an office answered and now needs the lead's review, which material each question holds (holds stay on the affected material only), and take the one next step for the selected question. Constraints: PRODUCT.md and docs/redesign/visual-system.md; server commands, payloads, roles and safeguards unchanged (the query gains read-only derived fields only); office messages are simulated and labeled once where they matter; "Drafted" never reads as "Sent"; an answer never resolves and a resolution never releases a hold; no AI confidence, no invented rates; J/K, Enter, Esc, arrow keys and Ctrl+Enter keep working; the scan field's CLR lookup still opens `?selected=`.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A question is read as the exchange it is (what the lab asked, what reached the office, what the office said, what the lead decided) beside a queue ordered by urgency, with the one next step docked. It refuses the ticket table with a side panel of stacked boxes.
OWN-WORLD: The shared H&E world, lent unchanged: hematoxylin ink and primary actions, eosin only for focus, cool bench-gray ground, white evidence sheets separated by hairlines, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for words and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono only for identifiers, the severity ladder (amber with words for overdue, delivery failed and delivery unknown; hatched ink for a hold with its scope; neutral with the owner's name for waiting, e.g. "Kim Nguyen to review"; quiet check for resolved; red only where the evidence shows a patient-identity difference, as in CLR-0453's date of birth), and the specimen-label signature: every queue row leads with the material's label reference (dashed for a provisional INT id), and the open question sets its material as the SpecimenLabel.
STORY: Kim opens the desk, sees the overdue and undelivered questions first in amber words, opens the identity question the office answered, reads the office's words under the question and the aligned order-versus-bottle evidence, writes her review note where the review belongs in the conversation and presses the docked "Mark answer reviewed"; the hold on the affected material stays in hatched ink until a lead releases it with a reason. Maya sees her own drafts and follow-ups; the director sees the same calm structure and who can act.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (lead's). White job header: Clarifications, six saved filters with counts in one row, the simulated-messages note. Left queue (320 to 352px, canvas): rows of LabelRef and part, the question type in words, the ladder flag and hold chip, the follow-up time, the CLR id. Record: identity band (SpecimenLabel md, the question type at lg with the CLR id and state, patient marked synthetic, office, owner) and a one-sentence status band on the ladder with the hold chip; then the conversation sheet as a lifecycle spine (question as drafted or as sent with the aligned evidence, notices with their simulated delivery outcomes, the office answer, the lead's review, the resolution, future steps shown as "not yet"), the hold, office coverage, history. Decision column (300 to 352px): Your next step (heading names the action; fields live in the conversation where their content will sit; the commit is docked with its Ctrl+Enter badge or disabled with its reason), Waiting on, Also possible, cancel. Below 1280px the queue becomes a drawer while a question is open; below 1024px (and at 200% zoom) the desk stacks and the page scrolls.
FORM: Extends the Intake world. User-pinned: the user's instruction for this redesign reads "Focus first on making the accessioning workstation excellent. Keep the active case and specimen identity visible, align evidence for comparison, and make the next action clear. Then extend the selected design language to the other screens." The selected language is the Intake H&E workstation (concept A, chosen on the brief's ordering). No concept-seed roll ran for this screen, by design: the user asked for the extension of one selected language, not a new direction. Screen-specific form: the conversation drawn as a lifecycle spine whose open slot holds the next contribution (draft text, the answer given by phone, the review note), with the commit docked beside it.
FINISH: Captured at 1440, 1366 and 1024 and at 200% zoom (720x450, 683x384) in every required state (default list as Kim; CLR-0457 drafted; CLR-0453 answered awaiting review; CLR-0454 delivery failed; CLR-0451 delivery unknown; a resolved one; an empty tab; read-only director; Maya's My open), looked at, fixed and recaptured once; no horizontal scroll and no console errors; tsc and the clarifications domain tests green; the e2e clarification steps updated to the new structure at equal strength.
