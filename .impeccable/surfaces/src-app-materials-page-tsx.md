# Materials bench (surface brief)

Scope: `/materials` with `?tab=print|trays|retrieval` and `?selected=<PJ|TRY|RR id>` (URLs unchanged), mode **Operate**. Audience: histotechnologists at the bench with a slide or cassette in one hand and the scanner in the other (Priya Shah: labels and trays; Marcus Lee: block retrieval); supervisors release with exceptions; directors watch it in the demo (read only). Tasks: print slide/block labels and reconcile an unknown print outcome before any reprint; assemble a slide tray by scanning each slide into its position (an unexpected slide is flagged, completion is refused with reasons); retrieve blocks from their last confirmed location with a scan check that rejects the wrong block and changes nothing. Constraints: PRODUCT.md and docs/redesign/visual-system.md; server commands, payloads, roles and safeguards unchanged; printers and scanners labeled simulated once where they matter; locations are last confirmed, never live; no confidence numbers, no invented rates.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Each tab is drawn as the physical thing in the hand: the tray as its slots, the label as the label, the location as the walk to the slot. It refuses the table-of-rows-for-everything and the stack of equal cards.
OWN-WORLD: The shared H&E world, lent unchanged: hematoxylin ink and primary actions, eosin only for focus, cool bench-gray ground, white evidence sheets, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for words and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono only for identifiers, the severity ladder (amber with words for unexpected or missing material, hatched ink for holds, neutral with a name for waiting, quiet check for done, red never used here), and the specimen-label signature: a slot shows the slide's own label end, a print job shows its label preview.
STORY: Priya reads the tray as it sits on the bench, sees which positions are still empty and which slide does not belong, and the column beside it says in one sentence why the tray cannot be completed and what each item needs. Marcus reads the path to the slot and who last confirmed it, scans, and a wrong block is refused in words.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (lead's). A slim job header (Materials, three job tabs, simulated note). Trays: tray band (tray label, dermatopathologist, progress, status), the slot grid with the scan cue above it and a side column beside or below it (material on the tray but not on its list first, in its own amber lane, then the simulated bench), and a fixed decision column (blocked sentence at lg, the items to resolve with their actions, Mark tray complete with its reason). Retrieval: request band, blocks grouped by storage path (each stop says once that locations are last confirmed, not live), decision column with the walk to the next block (box path, then the slot set large with who last confirmed it), the scan check and its result. Print: printers, the job queue with outcomes in words, the selected job with its label preview, the reconcile step, then the reprint gate with its reason.
FORM: Extends the Intake world. User-pinned: the user's instruction for this redesign reads "Focus first on making the accessioning workstation excellent. Keep the active case and specimen identity visible, align evidence for comparison, and make the next action clear. Then extend the selected design language to the other screens." The selected language is the Intake H&E workstation (concept A, chosen on the brief's ordering). No concept-seed roll ran for this screen, by design: the user asked for extension of one selected language, not a new direction. Screen-specific form: the tray drawn as an occupancy grid bound to real slots; retrieval by last confirmed location (the lead's composition for this job, from the baseline review in docs/redesign/references-and-skills.md).
FINISH: Captured at 1440, 1366 and 1024 in every required state, looked at, fixed and recaptured once; tsc and the materials domain tests green; e2e assertions for steps 6 and 7 updated to the new wording at equal strength.

## Round 2 decisions (2026-10-14)
- Bench frame only at 1024px wide and more than 560px tall: the record band and the decision column are pinned there and nowhere else. Below that (200% zoom, portrait tablet) the page is one scrolling stack in DOM and tab order: band, decision (tray: why it cannot be completed and the items; retrieval: the next block, its walk, still to pick, Deliver), then the material. Only the top bar stays pinned. The tray picker becomes one sideways-scrolling row so the tray in hand is in the first view.
- A pinned decision column fits the viewport below its own top (the band can wrap after a scan) and never shows half a line or half a button: a cut unit is covered and the cue says "N more items/blocks below" (or "More below" for the rest of one item). Item actions come before their long sentence.
- Retrieval: "Still to pick (N)" lists the requested blocks in walking order (the next block first) with the simulated scanner's Scan; the look-alike wrong block sits apart, marked "Not on this request". "Still to pick:" as a sentence remains only where that list is not shown (read only).
- Tray side column: beside the tray on a bench tablet the off-list lane lays its slides on their side; under the tray on wider screens the lane and the bench sit side by side, so the bench's first action is always in the first view.
- Shared parts replace local workarounds: IdText for identifiers in sentences, the shared selected-row outline, LabelPreview's own wrapping, Button size="block". Unavailable printers are amber (a source state), not red.
