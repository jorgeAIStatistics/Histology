# Worklist (surface brief)

Scope: `/worklist` in every view (Histotech, Grossing and source entry, Accessioning, Operations), its side panel, loading state and printed snapshot. Mode **Operate**. Audience: histotechs at the bench (Priya Shah is the reference user), the grosser and histology assistant in their views, accessioners; the supervisor and director read the Operations view, the director read only. Task: the digital replacement for the printed daily worklist ("the bible", colored by doctor, PAS in yellow): which cases, parts, blocks and slides are mine, which dermatopathologist each belongs to, which need PAS, what is held and on what scope, what changed since I last acknowledged or printed. Constraints: PRODUCT.md (doctor color always with the name, PAS always written, holds name their scope, simulated and stale stated, keyboard J/K/Enter/A/P and Esc, find-in-list by scanner wedge), the visual system (`docs/redesign/visual-system.md`), server safeguards untouched; shared parts used, not forked. Baseline: `docs/redesign/before/before-worklist-*.jpg` (generic stacked toolbars above a table, colored side stripes on doctor groups and holds).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The worklist is the bible made live: one sheet, one band per dermatopathologist, rows dense enough to read like the paper and honest enough to say when it is stale. It refuses the stacked filter toolbar above a generic data table.
OWN-WORLD: The shared H&E world, lent unchanged: hematoxylin ink and primary actions, eosin only for focus, cool bench-gray ground, white evidence sheet; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for words, Mono only for identifiers; severity ladder (PAS yellow with the word, holds hatched ink with their scope, sources-disagree/stale amber, waiting neutral with the owner); the specimen label as the selected case's identity in the side panel.
STORY: Priya reads her doctor's band, sees the yellow PAS rows and the hatched holds without hunting, opens a row to see its parts, blocks and slides as labels, and prints a snapshot that carries its version and data age; the director reads the same sheet in the Operations view.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Header line (Worklist, view switch, All cases scope, Saved filters, Print snapshot with its P key, CSV). One toolbar line on the sheet (find, doctor, stage, PAS only / On hold / My work / Changed). The change line under it is the state of this copy, one line at every bench width: version and read time with the source data age in the same words everywhere ("Simulated source as of …", amber with ": out of date" when stale; the banner above says what is paused), the row count, what changed since acknowledgment (led by the version on the tablet) with the last print under it, and Acknowledge with A. Then the grid: sticky column header, sticky doctor band on a neutral ground (doctor colour as a 4 px top rule, swatch and colour word; yellow on the sheet is PAS only; name, case, PAS and hold counts), dense rows that read on one line (every column sized to its usual content, Readiness takes the rest; PAS always with its work order, "PAS (WO-2141)"; Stage folded into "Parts and stage"; empty cells a quiet dash; only simulated-source updates marked, with the plug; a provisional item as its dashed label with the word "Provisional"). Tablet (< 1280): the P1 columns only (Source entry stays in the Grossing view); the rest is in the side panel. Side panel: the case as its printed label with its DataMatrix (provisional items dashed, no code), heading for screen readers only (hideTitle); J/K with the panel open keeps focus on the row, Enter moves it to the panel. 200% zoom (720x450, 683x384): the page stacks and scrolls, the grid's box is one window tall under the top bar with its Case column sticky, and a focused row is never under the sticky header or band.
FORM: Extends the Intake world. User-pinned: the user's instruction for this redesign reads "Focus first on making the accessioning workstation excellent. Keep the active case and specimen identity visible, align evidence for comparison, and make the next action clear. Then extend the selected design language to the other screens." The selected language is the Intake H&E workstation (concept A, chosen on the brief's ordering). No concept-seed roll ran for this screen, by design: the user asked for extension of one selected language, not a new direction. Screen-specific form: the doctor-grouped grid as the main surface with one consolidated toolbar (the lead's composition for this job, from the baseline review in docs/redesign/references-and-skills.md).
FINISH: Captured at 1440, 1366 and 1024 in every listed state and at 200% zoom, looked at and corrected, tsc and the worklist domain tests green, e2e assertions updated to the new wording at equal or greater strength.
