# Histology Redesigned

**A one-page pitch: measure first, then decide**

*Jorge Rodriguez, histology assistant · 2026-10-14 · For the Director of Operations and Medical Director*

> Nothing here has been endorsed or approved by anyone. The demo uses only made-up data and has never touched EMA, the lab network, or patient information.

---

### 1. Why I'm raising this

In about four months at the bench, plus what I saw at Quest and UHealth, I keep seeing the same thing: **we copy the same information by hand at several handoffs.**

- Accessioners remember which office goes to which dermatopathologist.
- Parts A and B sometimes arrive as separate orders, and are hard to fix after accessioning.
- Questions to offices can wait a long time, with no clear owner for the follow-up.
- Gross descriptions are typed into EMA in an end-of-day batch; some nights that runs to about 9 p.m.
- More than 300 slide labels may be printed and applied by hand on a given day, and "the bible" is printed and color-coded by hand.

These are **my observations, not measured numbers.** I'd like to measure them properly.

### 2. The idea in one sentence

**Histology Lab OS is a companion to EMA, not a replacement:** it coordinates the handoffs between people and systems, while EMA keeps accessions, orders, gross reports and diagnoses.

### 3. What the five-minute demo shows

One made-up case, step by step:

- An 11 a.m. courier delivery is checked against what was expected. Known offices are routed by a written rule; an unknown office is flagged, not guessed.
- Parts A and B arrive as two orders. The system **suggests** they may be related; a person decides. Nothing is merged.
- Part B's bottle says *left* forearm and the order says *right*. A clarification is opened and **only Part B** is held.
- The grosser finishes Part A, and the next task and the doctor/PAS worklist update right away while Part B stays visibly unfinished.
- A tray missing a slide can't be marked complete. A printer timeout is shown as "unknown," not "printed."
- A wrong block scan at retrieval is rejected.

Every outside connection in the demo is simulated and labeled.

### 4. Configure first, buy second, build last

1. **EMA first.** ModMed advertises gross defaults, configurable stains, tracking and label printing. We don't yet know what we have turned on. A configuration change may be the cheapest and safest fix.
2. **Existing products second.** Tracking, grossing documentation and slide-printing products already exist.
3. **Build only what's left**, and only after measuring.

No prices are in this pitch. Every cost would need a real quote.

### 5. What I'm asking for

**Yes, please:**
- Permission to observe and time a few real workflow cycles, with my coworkers' agreement and no patient identifiers in my notes.
- Thirty minutes for a small group to review the synthetic demo and tell me what I got wrong.
- Help getting a few questions answered by ModMed (what's licensed, what's configurable, what interfaces exist).

**Not asking for:** purchases, budget, system access, patient data, installing anything, or contacting any office.

### 6. How we'd know if it helps

- Accession minutes per case, clarification response and resolution times, grossing-to-entry delay, age of unfinished work, retyping time, reprints, paper pages, label handling time, block retrieval time.
- Always the median **and** the slow cases, with unfinished work shown, not hidden.
- We also count the **extra checking** any new tool adds. Time saved, waiting reduced, and actual dollars are kept separate.

### 7. What this is not

Not a validated system. Not compliant just because it has role buttons or a disclaimer. Not a claim to have invented specimen tracking or AI histology. It is a concrete, testable idea, and I'd like the chance to test it the right way.
