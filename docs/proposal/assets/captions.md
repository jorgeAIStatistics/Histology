Prepared by Jorge Rodriguez for the Director of Operations, Platinum Dermatology. Every image comes from the synthetic demonstration on a local laptop: fictional laboratory ("Lumen Bay Dermatopathology Laboratory (fictional)"), fictional staff, offices and patients, and simulated connectors. Nothing here came from EMA, a lab network or patient information. People named in the images are fictional demo roles.

Captured 2026-10-24 from a production build in demo mode (`HLOS_MODE=demo`, its own build folder and database, port 3510), after **Reset demo**, by clicking the eight guided steps on the real screens as the named demo roles (the path in `tests/e2e/director-journey.spec.ts`). Chromium, 1440 × 900 at 2× pixel density (2880 × 1800 PNG), reduced motion, light theme. Notifications and the scan field's result pop-up were closed before each capture; the same message stays visible on the page.
