# Redesign decision log

*Dated 2026-10-14. Each entry gives the decision, why it was made, and who chose it. "User" means the author's answers in chat.*

| # | Decision | Why | By |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treat the reference sites (costanera.uk, reveallabs.co) as a level-of-care reference only, and copy nothing. | Brand commitment in PRODUCT.md. The in-app browser refused both sites. Design Extract was then done by hand with curl, because its `memi` command is not installed. | User + lead |
| 2 | Render three Intake concepts on one fixture (A precision workstation, B specimen atlas, C guided desk) and pick A. | The brief preferred A on a tie. Independent walkthrough scores: A 10/12. From the others, keep C's finding band and step rail and B's effect preview. | Brief + lead |
| 3 | Visual world: H&E stain. Hematoxylin for structure, eosin only for focus and the brand mark, a cool bench-gray ground, white evidence sheets. | It is specific to histology. It avoids the generic SaaS palette, the cream-and-serif look and the dark acid-accent look. The stain's own two colors give a natural primary and a sparing accent. | Lead |
| 4 | Type: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for words, Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for identifiers only. | Both are designed to tell look-alike characters apart (0/O, 1/l/I). A misread identifier is the real risk at a specimen bench, so the font choice serves function, not costume. | Lead |
| 5 | Signature: the part in hand is shown as its specimen label. It is dashed while provisional and solid with its DataMatrix once official. | Identity has to stay visible, and a provisional id must never look official. The label answers both the way the bench already does. | Lead |
| 6 | Severity ladder: identity red; sources disagree or data may be wrong amber; hold hatched ink; waiting neutral with the owner's name; PAS yellow only. | The critique found one orange used for everything, which under-weighted the patient-identity case. | Critique (user chose to fix all four P1s) |
| 7 | Keep missing container and source offline on amber. Do not give them a separate rung as the finish review suggested. | Both mean the data may be wrong (manifest vs arrival; stale source). A fifth colour would dilute the ladder. Routing exception moved to the neutral rung, because it needs a person, not a correction. | Lead (documented in visual-system.md) |
| 8 | Ctrl+Enter runs the badged step only. It never records a container check, and the target is always visible. | Recording the check is an attestation, so it must be a deliberate act. | User |
| 9 | One provenance key, plus labels where they matter. | Repeating "Provisional"/"Synthetic" on every row was noise; the detector flagged it. | User |
| 10 | Replace the left icon rail with a hematoxylin top bar carrying job navigation, the scan field, source freshness, an always-visible "Synthetic demo" pill and one presenter menu (role switch, demo clock, guided demo, settings, keys). | The finish review: a white bar over a navy icon rail is the stock admin-template shell, and contradicted the contract. Bonus: 84px more width for every bench screen, and the 1024 overflow is fixed at its root. | Finish review + lead |
| 11 | The presenter menu hides the role switch behind one click. Three tests now open the menu to read the role. | Presenter controls should not sit on bench screens. Assertions keep the same strength. | Lead |
| 12 | Raise the minimum text size from 12px to 13px (`--text-xs`). | Bench reading distance. Pills, captions, keyboard hints and timeline details were 12px. | Lead (from the Intake builder's report) |
| 13 | No coloured side stripes anywhere. Holds use a hatch, the selected table row uses a fill plus an outline, and notes use a full border. | Craft floor ban. Stripes were one of the admin-template tells. | Impeccable craft floor |
| 14 | Below 1024px (tablet portrait, 200% zoom) the top bar wraps to two rows, and the identity bar stops being sticky on short viewports. | Reflow without horizontal scroll; pinned chrome must not hide the focused control (WCAG 2.2: 1.4.10, 2.4.11). | Lead |
| 15 | Record, but do not change, the command-receipt replay path: it is not bound to actor, scope or payload. | The brief said to keep server safeguards untouched and to put production work in a separate backlog. | Brief |
| 16 | Propose, but do not build, a separate director presentation surface. | The brief asked for a proposal only. | Brief |
| 17 | Use motion only for state changes: a row arriving, content swapping, a scan locating a row. Durations are 120/200/320ms and reduced motion is respected. | The user wanted the app to feel alive, and Impeccable and Frontend Design both rule out ambient decoration. | User + skills |
| 18 | Red only for a patient-identity conflict between sources, and for destructive confirmation buttons. A scanned label from another case is amber, named in words, and rejected without changing anything. Failures, unknown outcomes, an offline source and invalid form values are also amber. | The ladder has to stay legible. Wrong-case scans are already blocked, so they are a mismatch to resolve, not a patient-identity conflict. | Lead (visual-system.md) |
| 19 | Show "done" as a quiet check in muted ink, never green. Use dashed outlines only for things that are not yet real: a provisional id, an unsent draft, an empty slot. | Green confirmations and dashed honesty tags blurred the signals that matter. Reviewers found this on four screens. | Lead, from the reviews |
| 20 | Promote the hatched hold chip, the Ctrl+Enter badge, the presenter-controls disclosure, the filled-octagon identity mark, IdText and LabelRef to shared parts. Delete the screen copies. | One lead owns consistency. Three screens had each drawn the same parts their own way. | Lead |
| 21 | The tray header shows unscanned slides as neutral progress ("1 left to scan"). Only real exceptions are amber. Delivery holds are named in the decision column, which says that completion is allowed and Deliver is refused. | The reviewer found amber on ordinary progress. The server blocks delivery, not completion, when a slide is held (`src/server/domain/tray.ts`). | Lead, from the review |
| 22 | When the lead's related-order decision is the next step (1280px and wider), the centre leads with the two submissions side by side and a wide decision band. | Independent re-critique, P1: at 1366 the decision did not fit the side column. | Re-critique |
| 23 | The presenter button shows the demo time over the name. The role is in the menu and in the button's accessible name. | This gave 67px back to the scan field, so the Intake hint no longer gets cut off at 1366 or 1440. | Lead |
| 24 | Toasts appear bottom-left on bench screens, and top-right under the bar on stacked screens. | They covered docked actions and side panels on the right. The trade-off: they briefly cover the bottom of the Intake queue. | Lead |
