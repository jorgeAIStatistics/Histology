# Five-minute demo script

*DermPath Research Workbench (working name). Research use only. For a five-minute meeting with the medical director, presented by Jorge Rodriguez.*

The UI is being built now. Every on-screen label in this script is marked **[verify against the built UI]** and must be checked in a dry run. The API calls in each step come from implementation-plan.md section 6 and are listed so that the presenter knows what each action really does. Navigation: **Worklist, Slide Review, Dataset, Experiments, Impact**.

## Before the meeting (not part of the five minutes)

- [ ] Reset the demo data: `POST /api/v1/demo/reset` (admin role, demo mode only). Button: "Reset demo" [verify against the built UI].
- [ ] Confirm the fictional case below exists in the seed, with parts A and B, a recorded QC issue, a pending label and a disputed slide [verify against `seed.py`].
- [ ] Pre-run one synthetic experiment so a completed run is ready (training runs as a queued job and should not be waited on live).
- [ ] Confirm the Impact screen opens with its **fictional** defaults labelled as fictional.
- [ ] Check every step below against the readiness matrix. **Drop any step whose feature is not built and tested.** Do not demo a placeholder as if it works.
- [ ] Desktop at 1440 px or wider. Close every other window and tab. **No real patient material is open on this machine.**

## 0:00–0:15. Opening caveat (say this first)

> "Before I start: this is a research-use-only prototype. Everything you will see is either public test data released under CC0 or synthetic and fictional. Nothing here uses our lab's data, and nothing implies that you or the lab endorse it. The software does not make diagnoses, and nothing on screen claims diagnostic capability."

## 0:15–1:15. Why this matters (60 seconds)

Say the 60-second version from director-overview.md, then the commitment in one line:

> "The commitment is that you decide: you can accept, correct, reject or hide anything the software suggests, you can record your own view before seeing any suggestion, and nothing is signed out, ordered, dismissed or reprioritised automatically. Any extra work for histology staff gets measured, not hidden."

## 1:15–1:50. Worklist: a fictional case with two specimen parts

**Screen:** Worklist. Role: histology assistant, using the demo role switch [verify against the built UI].

**Do:** Open fictional case **S-DEMO-0142**, coded patient **CP-0007** [verify the seeded IDs]. Expand the hierarchy: **Part A** (fictional: shave biopsy, upper back) and **Part B** (fictional: punch biopsy, nasal area), each with a block and H&E slides at several levels.

**Say:**
> "This is a made-up case. The hierarchy goes patient code, case, part, block, slide, level and stain. That matters later: all of one patient's slides must stay together when we build a dataset. There are no names here, only codes. Note the provenance tag: these images are **DEMO SYNTHETIC**, generated by software, with no real tissue in them."

**Optional, 10 s:** open the **PUBLIC CC0** slide to show a real tissue image with pan and zoom. Say: "This one is a real public test slide. It is unlabeled, so we draw no diagnostic conclusion from it."

**Behind it:** `GET /projects/{pid}/worklist`, `GET /cases/{id}`. The demo role switch is `POST /auth/demo-login`, and the UI should say it is **not authentication**.

## 1:50–2:30. Slide Review: a QC issue

**Screen:** Slide Review, Part B, level 2 [verify against the seed].

**Do:** Open the QC panel [verify against the built UI]. Show the automated screen (tissue found, rough blur indicator), both marked **experimental**. Show a **fold** issue recorded by staff and walk its status: open → reviewed → suggested action (for example "rescan" or "recut", as a suggestion) → resolved → **dataset eligibility** (for example excluded, with a reason).

**Say:**
> "Only tissue detection and a rough blur screen are automated, and both are experimental. Folds, chatter and staining problems are recorded by staff. A flag never orders a recut or throws anything away. The point is the link: issue, what we did about it, the outcome, and whether the slide may be used for research."

**Behind it:** `GET /slides/{id}/qc`, `POST /slides/{id}/qc/issues`, `PATCH /qc/issues/{id}` (review, action, resolution, override with a reason).

## 2:30–3:10. Expert review and an approved label

**Screen:** Slide Review, Part A. Switch role to pathologist [verify against the built UI].

**Do:**
1. With independent review on, the suggestion area is hidden. Record an assessment first [verify the control label].
2. Reveal the suggestion. It is fictional seed data. Choose **accept, correct, reject or hide** [verify the labels].
3. Approve the label at **slide** level: "BCC present" or "BCC absent (may include other malignancy, inflammation or mimics)" [verify the wording on screen].
4. Open the disagreements list and show one disputed slide with two different reader labels awaiting adjudication.

**Say:**
> "This is fictional data on a synthetic image, so no diagnosis is being made. What matters is the record: your own assessment is saved before you see a suggestion, the suggestion and your decision are stored separately, and every change has an author, a time and a reason. Disagreements stay visible until a pathologist settles them."

**Behind it:** `POST /slides/{id}/assessment`, `GET /slides/{id}/labels`, `POST /labels/{id}/review`, `POST /slides/{id}/labels`, `POST /slides/{id}/review-state`, `GET /projects/{pid}/disagreements`, `POST /disagreements/{id}/adjudicate`.

## 3:10–3:40. Dataset: a frozen version

**Screen:** Dataset. Role: research lead [verify against the built UI].

**Do:** Show the summary: **unique patients counted separately from slides**, label balance, exclusions (the Part B slide, with its QC reason), missing metadata, reader agreement. Create patient-grouped splits, then **Freeze** [verify the button label]. Show the manifest hash and that the test split is locked.

**Say:**
> "Freezing records the exact image hashes, label versions and split assignments. If a label changes later, we get a new version; the old one never changes. Every slide from one patient lands on the same side, so the model is never tested on a patient it has already seen. The test set is locked away from training and tuning."

**Behind it:** `GET /datasets/{id}/summary`, `POST /datasets/{id}/splits`, `POST /datasets/{id}/freeze`, `GET /datasets/{id}/manifest`.

## 3:40–4:20. Experiments: a reproducible run and error inspection

**Screen:** Experiments. Open the pre-run experiment.

**Do:**
1. Point to the banner **PIPELINE TEST — NOT PATHOLOGY VALIDATION** [verify it appears on every result view].
2. Show what makes it reproducible: dataset version and manifest hash, seed, code revision, dependency versions, checkpoint.
3. Show the simple baseline (mean-pooled embeddings with logistic regression) next to attention-based MIL.
4. Open error inspection: the confusion matrix **with denominators**, any metric shown as undefined, and the error list [verify the labels].
5. Open the encoder list: a pathology encoder shown as **missing weights**, not silently replaced [verify against `GET /encoders`].

**Say:**
> "This run uses synthetic numbers to prove the machinery works: data in, model trained, results saved and repeatable. It says nothing about pathology performance. When the denominator is too small, the screen says so instead of showing a number. The more complex model has to beat the simple one to earn its place. And the pathology encoder is shown as unavailable because we have not obtained licensed weights; we don't swap in something else under the same name."

**Behind it:** `GET /projects/{pid}/experiments`, `GET /experiments/{id}`, `GET /experiments/{id}/evaluation?split=val`, `GET /experiments/{id}/errors`, `GET /encoders`. Starting a new run is `POST /projects/{pid}/experiments`; show it only if time allows, and do not wait for it.

## 4:20–4:50. Impact: adjustable economic impact

**Screen:** Impact.

**Do:** Point to the **fictional** label on the defaults. Change two inputs live, for example minutes saved per case and the realisation fraction, and lower the minutes saved until the annual net benefit turns **negative** [verify the result display]. Point out that technologist time and new scanning and annotation costs are inputs too.

**Say:**
> "These numbers are made up; real ones would come from timing our own work. Freed time is not automatically cash, so this setting controls how much of it counts. If the extra scanning and annotation outweigh the time saved, the calculator shows a loss, and we would report that. It doesn't assume we have a scanner, and it counts freed capacity, not fewer pathologists."

**Behind it:** the client-side ROI calculator (formulas from the brief, tested with vitest).

## 4:50–5:00. Close and the ask

> "What I'm asking for is your review of a one-page pilot protocol and a data governance template. Nothing goes further without the right approvals, and the lab's data stays out of this prototype."

Hand over pilot-protocol.md, data-governance.md and director-overview.md.

## Phrases to avoid

| Avoid | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "The AI diagnoses / detects BCC." | "The prototype records expert labels. No model has been trained on real slides." |
| "It's X% accurate." (about any synthetic result) | "This is a pipeline test on synthetic data. It says nothing about pathology performance." |
| "Validated", "clinically validated", "clinical-grade" | "Engineering-tested. Not validated on pathology." |
| "FDA-ready", "HIPAA-compliant", "CLIA/CAP-ready" | "We make no regulatory or compliance claims. The governance template lists what would be needed." |
| "It will replace / reduce pathologists." Also "AI can never replace doctors." | "Our commitment is to build it to augment pathologists. Whether it saves time is what the pilot measures." |
| "It saves N hours" or "it pays back in N months" | "With these fictional inputs the calculator shows this. Real inputs come from timing our work." |
| "In partnership with the lab", "endorsed by", "the director supports" | "I'm exploring a possible research pilot, subject to approval." |
| "Our slides", "our data" | "Public CC0 test data and synthetic images." |
| "Better than [vendor]", "nobody else does QC / skin AI", "first", "unique" | "Others already offer QC and skin AI. We are testing a narrower workflow idea." |
| "The heatmap shows the tumour." | "That is an attention map. It is not a tumour outline or an explanation." |
| "It automatically finds folds and chatter." | "Staff record those. Only tissue detection and a rough blur screen are automated, and both are experimental." |
| "BCC absent means benign." | "BCC absent can still include other cancers, inflammation or look-alikes." |
| "It will triage / prioritise cases." | "It does not reprioritise anything." |
| "This is worth $X" (any valuation) | "A working demo shows we can build. It does not establish a valuation." |

## If something fails during the demo

- Say what failed in plain words, and move to the next step. Do not improvise a claim to cover the gap.
- A button that cannot work should explain its missing dependency on screen. Read that explanation aloud; it is part of the honesty of the product.
