# Pilot protocol template (one page, for the medical director's review)

*DermPath Research Workbench, research use only. A draft for discussion, not an approved protocol. Placeholders read [to confirm]. No step begins before the authorisations in row 2 are in place.*

| Element | Protocol entry |
|---|---|
| **1. Task and population** | Research task: **BCC present vs BCC absent** on H&E skin biopsy slides. "BCC absent" is **not** "benign": it may include SCC, melanoma, other malignancy, inflammation and mimics. Indeterminate, inadequate and out-of-scope are allowed outcomes. Population: [to confirm: specimen types, sites, age range, date window, exclusions]. Label level stated per record (case, slide or region). |
| **2. Authorisations** | Data access: [to confirm]. Research review (IRB or equivalent): [to confirm]. De-identification sign-off: [to confirm]. Data use agreement, including rights in annotations, derived models, publication and commercialisation: [to confirm] (see data-governance.md). Commercialisation decisions: [to confirm]. |
| **3. Feasibility** | Scanner availability: [to confirm: in-house, borrowed or service; capacity; who scans]. Label feasibility: [to confirm: source of truth, slide or case level]. Annotation time: measured per case in a run-in; budget [to confirm: pathologist minutes per week]. Extra scanning and QC work for histology staff is **measured**, not absorbed silently. |
| **4. Case selection** | **Consecutive eligible cases** from [to confirm: period] for representative evaluation. **Enriched difficult cases** (mimics, small or superficial BCC, poor preparation) tracked as a **separate** set and never pooled into the representative estimates. |
| **5. Ground truth** | Reference standard: [to confirm: signed-out diagnosis plus independent review]. Two independent readers blinded to each other and to any model output; disagreements adjudicated by [to confirm: third pathologist or consensus]. Disagreement rates reported. |
| **6. Data separation** | **Patient-separated** development splits (all specimens, levels and stains from one patient stay together). An **untouched** external-site or later-period test set, locked before development and opened once. Thresholds frozen on validation data first. |
| **7. Endpoints (prespecified)** | Primary: sensitivity and specificity for BCC present on the locked test set, with 95% CIs (Wilson or exact; patient-cluster bootstrap when patients contribute several slides). Secondary: PPV, NPV, abstention rate and errors at a fixed coverage; calibration; QC-issue traceability; staff time per slide and per case. |
| **8. Sample size from precision** | Positives needed: **n = z² · p(1 − p) / d²**, where *p* is the expected sensitivity, *d* the desired CI half-width and z = 1.96 for 95%. Illustrative only: p = 0.90, d = 0.05 → 139; p = 0.90, d = 0.10 → 35; p = 0.80, d = 0.05 → 246. **n depends on both the precision wanted and the expected sensitivity; there is no fixed "enough slides".** Divide by BCC prevalence among eligible cases [to confirm from a baseline audit] for total consecutive cases; inflate for clustering (design effect 1 + (m − 1)ρ). The same logic applies to specificity on BCC-absent cases. This approximation is poor near 0 or 1 and at small n; final intervals use exact or Wilson methods. |
| **9. Time and motion** | Baseline measurement of current tasks (QC recording, slide handling, review, annotation) before any tool use, for [to confirm: weeks]. Matched comparison of the same task types and case mix where possible. **Learning effects:** a run-in period excluded from analysis. **Order effects:** randomised or counterbalanced order of conditions. |
| **10. Reader-study endpoint** | **Physician-with-tool vs physician-without-tool** on the same cases, in randomised order with a washout of [to confirm], recording errors against the reference standard, time per case and workload [to confirm: scale]. Independent initial review records each reader's assessment before any suggestion is shown. **Model-only AUROC does not establish augmentation.** |
| **11. Shadow mode** | Any model output is produced **in shadow mode only**: not shown to the signing pathologist and with **no influence on clinical care**, until separate appropriate authorisation exists. No sign-out, ordering, dismissal or reprioritisation. |
| **12. Negative findings, stop and revise** | All results are reported, including negative ones. **Stop or revise if** [to confirm thresholds]: lower CI bound of sensitivity below the prespecified bound; annotation or scanning time exceeds the budget; de-identification or access failures; readers' errors or time worsen with the tool; the medical director or review body asks to stop. |
